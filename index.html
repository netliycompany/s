What is evaporation?

Evaporation is the process in which a liquid changes into a gas. For example, when the Sun warms water in a puddle, some of the water turns into invisible water vapour and rises into the air.

Evaporation also helps wet clothes dry. Heat makes evaporation happen faster. It is an important part of the water cycle: water evaporates, cools to form clouds, and can later fall back to Earth as rain.
